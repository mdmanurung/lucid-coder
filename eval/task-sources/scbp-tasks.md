# Eval-task candidates from Single-cell Best Practices

Source: the live book at https://www.sc-best-practices.org/ and its notebook source, `theislab/single-cell-best-practices` at commit `dd6eb86` (2026-10-19).
I read the notebooks, not only the rendered pages, to get exact loader calls and printed outputs; one live page was spot-checked and matches.
Book code licence: Apache-2.0 (repo `LICENSE`). **No chapter states a dataset licence**, so every licence cell below reads "not stated".

Most chapters load data through lamindb: `ln.connect("theislab/sc-best-practices")`, then `ln.Artifact.get(key=...).load()`.
The original public loader comes from `scripts/<chapter>/*_dataset.ipynb` in the repo and is listed where it exists.
"Size" is the HTTP `content-length` I measured, or a lamin artifact `size=` printed in the notebook; the book text never states sizes.

## Ranked candidates

Ranked by how clean the trap is, then by how small the dataset can be made.

| # | Chapter / step | Dataset (loader) | Pitfall the chapter warns about | Recommended method (package) | Gate / known answer | Determinism |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Compositional](https://www.sc-best-practices.org/conditions/compositional/): cell-type abundance change, Salmonella vs Control | Haber 2017 regions, 9842 × 15215, 10 samples (`batch`), 8 cell types; `pt.dt.haber_2017_regions()`. Task only needs the 10 × 8 count table (KB) | "If we ignore the compositionality of the data, and use univariate methods like Wilcoxon rank-sum tests or scDC ... we may falsely perceive cell-type population shifts as statistically sound effects". The book attributes Haber's reported Stem/TA decrease to this: "all other cell types appear to decrease, even though this shift is only caused by the compositional properties of the data" | scCODA, `pt.tl.Sccoda()`, reference `Endocrine`, `formula="condition"`, FDR 0.2 (pertpy, Python) | Enterocyte up under Salmonella is a credible effect. Fail if the method is univariate per cell type (t-test, Wilcoxon, Poisson per type) or has no reference and no joint model. Do not gate on Stem/TA direction: the same chapter's Milo run finds Stem depletion and says "we don't have a ground truth" | NUTS with `rng_key=42`. The text states the direction of effects; full per-type credible table is truncated in output |
| 2 | [GSEA / pathways](https://www.sc-best-practices.org/conditions/gsea-pathway/): pathway activity from a DE contrast | Kang 2018 CD14+ monocyte pseudobulk DE table (2345 genes after filtering) + PROGENy weights; DE table key `conditions/differential_gene_expression/res_df_CD14_Monocytes.parquet`, PROGENy key `conditions/gsea_pathway/progeny.parquet` (or `dc.op.progeny(organism='human', top=100)`). The DE table regenerates without lamin from `pt.data.kang_2018()` plus the DE chapter code | "we recommend using `t-values` rather than `log2FCs`, as `t-values` account for the significance of the change". "Enrichment scores are signed: positive scores indicate pathway upregulation, while negative scores indicate downregulation" | `dc.mt.ulm(data=data, net=progeny)` on the `stat` column; `dc.mt.gsea` with Reactome sets of size 15-500 (decoupler, Python) | Significant (padj < 0.05): JAK-STAT = 17.146856, NFkB = 2.778471, both positive. Fail if the ranking uses p-value or abs(stat), or JAK-STAT is not top and positive | ULM is closed-form: deterministic. Plant: give `pvalue` and `stat` columns, so a p-value ranking loses the sign |
| 3 | [Surface protein QC](https://www.sc-best-practices.org/surface-protein/quality-control/): ADT cell filtering across samples | NeurIPS 2021 CITE-seq BMMC, 12 donors, 4 sites, 140 proteins; key `surface-protein/cite_filtered.h5mu` (1.4 GB for later stages). Public route: GEO `GSE194122_openproblems_neurips2021_cite_BMMC_processed.h5ad.gz` (616 MB, already cell-filtered by the challenge, so planted outliers may be needed). Subset `prot` to two samples, e.g. `s3d7` and `s4d8` | "As the total amount of reads and droplets can differ between the samples, a stringent, hard cutoff applied to all samples would not be appropriate." "If we compare sample s3d7 versus sample s4d8, we can see that the outliers of one sample would fit the regular distribution of normal counts in the other sample" | Per-sample MAD outliers (5 MADs on `log1p_total_counts` and `log1p_n_genes_by_counts`), loop over `obs["donor"]` (scanpy/muon, Python) | Full data: 122016 → 117390 (lenient global) → 106515 (per-sample). Gate: retained fraction per sample stays similar; a global cutoff guts the low-depth sample | Deterministic (MAD). Exact numbers on a subset must be regenerated |
| 4 | [Differential gene expression](https://www.sc-best-practices.org/conditions/differential-gene-expression/): does the IFN-β response differ between cell types? | Kang 2018 PBMC, 24673 × 15706, 8 patients × ctrl/stim; `pt.data.kang_2018()` (38 MB). Ship the 128 × 15701 pseudobulk | "Failing to account for the inherent correlation of replicates ... inflates the false discovery rate". The chapter tests a difference of responses with an interaction term `~ cell_type * label`. Pseudobulk filter `min_cells=10, min_counts=1000`: "All megakaryocyte pseudobulk samples failed quality control, and two CD8 T cell samples were removed" | `dc.pp.pseudobulk(..., mode="sum")`, `dc.pp.filter_samples`, `pt.tl.PyDESeq2(design="~ cell_type * label")` with an interaction contrast (decoupler + pertpy/PyDESeq2, Python) | RABGAP1L has a larger response in CD14+ monocytes than CD4 T cells; ENO1 has a smaller one. Fail if the answer compares two separate DE lists (significant in one, not in the other) or keeps megakaryocyte pseudobulks | PyDESeq2 is deterministic. Distinct trap from the existing pseudobulk task: the trap here is a difference of significance vs an interaction test |
| 5 | [Surface protein normalisation](https://www.sc-best-practices.org/surface-protein/normalization/): CLR on ADT counts | Same CITE-seq data; key `surface-protein/cite_quality_control.h5mu` (1.41 GB), or the GEO CITE file from #3. Ship `prot` counts only for a few thousand cells | "We recommend performing CLR normalization with axis=1, that is, normalizing each cell across proteins ... The alternative is to perform CLR normalization with axis=0 ... which is not generally desirable because proteins naturally exhibit substantial differences in abundance" | `mu.prot.pp.clr(mdata["prot"], axis=1)`; dsb if empty droplets and isotype controls exist (muon, Python) | Gate recomputes CLR both ways and checks the output matches axis=1. Plant: a prompt that says "normalise each protein" | Deterministic |
| 6 | [Pseudotemporal ordering](https://www.sc-best-practices.org/trajectories/pseudotemporal/): DPT with a root cell | Setty 2019 human bone marrow, 5780 × 27876; `sc.read(filename="bonemarrow.h5ad", backup_url="https://figshare.com/ndownloader/files/35826944")`. Size not stated (processed result artifact is 392 MB) | "it is only known that the developmental process starts in the form of hematopoietic stem cells, but not which cell, exactly". "Studying a bone marrow sample, for example, hematopoietic stem cells are assigned a low pseudotime, and erythroid cells a high pseudotime" | Root = `adata.obsm["X_diffmap"][:, 3].argmin()`, `sc.tl.dpt`; the book prefers Palantir (scanpy, palantir, Python) | Median pseudotime of HSC_1/HSC_2 below Ery_1/Ery_2. Fail if `iroot` is left at an arbitrary cell (e.g. 0) with no HSC justification. The book's own DPT is odd in CLP, so gate on HSC < Ery only | Diffmap is deterministic given the neighbour graph; the diffusion component index may shift between versions |
| 7 | [Compositional, Milo](https://www.sc-best-practices.org/conditions/compositional/): neighbourhood DA | Haber 2017 regions (as #1) | "to have a median of 3 cells from each sample in a neighbourhood, the number of cells in a neighbourhood should be at least 3 times the number of samples". With k=10 the median neighbourhood size is 25 (< 30) and is "underpowered" | `sc.pp.neighbors(n_neighbors=30, use_rep="X_scVI")`, `milo.make_nhoods(prop=0.1)`, `da_nhoods(design="~condition", model_contrasts="conditionSalmonella-conditionControl")` (pertpy, Python) | Gate: median neighbourhood size ≥ 30, and neighbours built on a batch-corrected space. Enterocyte neighbourhoods enriched under Salmonella | scVI training and nhood sampling are stochastic; gate on the size check, not exact counts |
| 8 | [Perturbation modelling](https://www.sc-best-practices.org/conditions/perturbation-modeling/): which guide-carrying cells are perturbed | Papalexi 2021 ECCITE-seq, 20729 cells, 26 targets, 3 replicates; `pt.dt.papalexi_2021()` (589 MB). Subset to NT plus a few targets | "many cells that received a targeting guide still express their target normally, and their inclusion dilutes the measured effects". "Cells of the same replicate are not independent samples, so we sum the counts of each group per replicate" | `pt.tl.Mixscape()` then pseudobulk + `PyDESeq2(design="~ replicate + mixscape_class")` (pertpy, Python) | KO fraction: JAK2 0.790, SMAD4 0.778, STAT1 0.761. IFNGR2 KO lowers IRF1, SOCS1, CXCL9, IL18BP. Fail if all guide carriers count as perturbed, or the DE uses cells as replicates | Mixscape uses kNN and a mixture model; KO fractions may move by a few points, so allow a tolerance |
| 9 | [Quality control, doublets](https://www.sc-best-practices.org/preprocessing-visualization/quality-control/): doublet calling on pooled samples | NeurIPS 2021 Multiome BMMC; the chapter uses one sample (s4d8, 16934 × 36601, key `preprocessing_visualization/quality_control_adata.h5ad`). A multi-sample task needs several samples from GEO GSE194122 | "Like ambient RNA correction, doublet detection has to be run per sample, as doublets only form between cells captured together." Ambient RNA "has to be run separately for every sample" | scDblFinder (R) via rpy2; Python fallback `sc.pp.scrublet(batch_key=...)` is my assumption, not in the chapter | Gate: doublet calls made per `sample` (batch key set, or per-sample loop). Single-sample QC answer: 16934 → 15998 cells after MAD filtering | Doublet simulation is random: gate on the per-sample structure, not on counts |
| 10 | [Annotation](https://www.sc-best-practices.org/cellular-structure/annotation/): CellTypist input | s4d8 after QC and clustering; key `cellular_structure/s4d8_clustered.h5ad`. Size not stated | Inferred from code, not a stated warning: the chapter resets `X` to raw counts, then `normalize_total(target_sum=10**4)` and `log1p` before CellTypist, although its `X` already holds scran log counts | `celltypist` with `Immune_All_Low.pkl` / `Immune_All_High.pkl` (Python) | Gate: matrix passed to CellTypist has expm1 row sums = 1e4. Plant: `X` holds scran-normalised values | Deterministic model; the trap is an input check |
| 11 | [Cell-cell communication](https://www.sc-best-practices.org/mechanisms/cell-cell-communication/): ligand-receptor inference | Kang 2018 (as #4); stim subset 12301 × 15701 | Run "on the data post IFN-beta stimulation alone, as such methods were initially designed for ... 'steady-state' data ... not across samples or conditions". LIANA prints "Assuming that counts were `natural` log-normalized!" | LIANA `rank_aggregate` / CellPhoneDB, `groupby="cell_type"`, `use_raw=False` (liana, Python) | Gate: one condition per run, input is log-normalised (not raw counts). No numeric known answer is stated | Permutation-based; set a seed |
| 12 | [Feature selection](https://www.sc-best-practices.org/preprocessing-visualization/feature-selection/): deviance HVGs | s4d8 normalised, 15998 × 20362; key `preprocessing_visualization/s4d8_normalization.h5ad` | "Unlike variance, deviance is defined on raw counts, so it needs no normalization first". For several batches, pass `batch` to `devianceFeatureSelection` "so that differences between batches do not make genes look deviant" | `scry::devianceFeatureSelection` via rpy2 (R) | Gate: deviance computed on the counts layer, with `batch` passed when the planted data has two batches | Closed form: deterministic |
| 13 | [Annotation, marker DE](https://www.sc-best-practices.org/cellular-structure/annotation/): cluster markers | s4d8 clustered (as #10) | "as the definition of the clusters is based on the same data as used for these statistical tests, the p-values of these tests will be inflated" | `sc.tl.rank_genes_groups` used for ranking only (scanpy) | Gate is textual: fail if marker p-values are reported as evidence of a discovery. Hard to gate in code | Deterministic |
| 14 | [ATAC QC](https://www.sc-best-practices.org/chromatin-accessibility/quality-control/): TSS enrichment threshold | 10x Multiome PBMC ~10k; keys `introduction/advanced_data_structures_and_frameworks_muon.h5mu` + fragments; the QC output artifact is 3.26 GB | "Its absolute value depends on the set of TSSs and on the tool, so thresholds from other pipelines do not transfer directly". "There are no universal thresholds" | `snap.metrics.tsse`, `snap.pp.filter_cells(min_counts=5000, max_counts=100000, min_tsse=10)`, `snap.pp.scrublet` (SnapATAC2, Python) | 11874 → 10255 cells with those thresholds. Gate on the threshold being read from the plotted distribution is weak | Deterministic filter; data too large for a small task |
| 15 | [Integration](https://www.sc-best-practices.org/cellular-structure/integration/): batch covariate choice | NeurIPS 2021 BMMC multiome, 69249 × 129921, subset to 10270 × 13431; `sc.read(filename="openproblems_bmmc_multiome_genes_filtered.h5ad", backup_url="https://figshare.com/ndownloader/files/45452260")` | "the choice of batch covariate indicates which level of variation should be retained and which level removed". "Using the patient as the batch covariate therefore risks removing real tumor biology" | scVI / scANVI / Scanorama / Harmony; evaluate with scib (Python) | No stated known answer; gate would check `batch_key` is not the condition of interest | scVI is stochastic |

Not worth a task: spatial SVGs (Moran's I on `sq.datasets.visium_hne_adata()`, 2688 × 18078) and neighbourhood enrichment, because the chapters warn only about library size and p-value underflow. RNA velocity (`scv.datasets.pancreas()`, 3696 × 27998) only warns not to over-read projections. Paired multimodal integration (WNN on NeurIPS CITE/multiome) has no gateable trap. The prediction section of perturbation modelling says to compare against a mean baseline, but Replogle K562 is 1.55 GB.
